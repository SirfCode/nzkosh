Data Safety

Plain-language summary of the current app

Current behaviour

  • No user account or sign-in.
  • No advertising or advertising SDK.
  • No analytics or behavioural tracking SDK.
  • No sale of personal information.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS or call-log access.
  • No user comments, uploads, payments or purchases.
  • Small technical diagnostics are stored locally on the device.
  • Diagnostics leave the device only when the user explicitly exports and shares them.
  • Audio playback sends ordinary network requests to GuruBox and third-party hosting services.

Android capabilities

  • Internet: load app content and stream audio.
  • Wake lock: maintain user-requested playback.
  • Foreground media playback service: continue audio with visible Android media controls.

Google Play Console guidance

The Play Console Data safety form must always match the released app and every included SDK. Based on the present implementation, the developer does not operate an account or analytics database and does not receive the locally stored diagnostic log unless a user deliberately shares it. Network service providers may process IP addresses and request metadata when serving audio.

Before submission, verify the current practices of every audio host and dependency. Reassess this statement if accounts, analytics, crash reporting, advertising, cloud diagnostics, comments, uploads or payments are added.
