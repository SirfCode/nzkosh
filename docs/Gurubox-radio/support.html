Support

Help for GuruBox Radio users

Playback problems

  • Confirm that the device has a working internet connection.
  • Try another radio or recording to determine whether one source is temporarily unavailable.
  • Keep battery optimisation from restricting GuruBox Radio during background playback.
  • Bluetooth disconnection, calls and other apps taking audio focus may pause playback.

Share diagnostics

Open the app menu, choose Diagnostics, mark the playback problem near the time it happens, and use Share. Review the destination before sending. The file contains technical playback events and should only be shared when support requests it.

Clear local information

Diagnostic logs can be cleared inside the Diagnostics screen. All app-local preferences and logs can also be removed from Android Settings by clearing GuruBox Radio storage, or by uninstalling the app.

Contact

For support and content questions, use the current contact information at gurubox.net. Include the installed app version, device model and a short description of what happened.
