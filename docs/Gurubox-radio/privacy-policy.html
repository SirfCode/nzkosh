Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026

GuruBox Radio provides internet radio, satsang, Vani and bhajan playback without requiring an account.

Information we collect

GuruBox Radio does not ask for or collect a user's name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, advertising identifier or payment information. The app does not include advertising, analytics, user accounts, comments or tracking SDKs.

Playback diagnostics

The app keeps a small rolling diagnostic log on the device to help investigate playback problems. It can contain technical playback states, errors, app lifecycle events, audio interruptions and explicit playback actions. It does not contain contacts, account details, location, advertising identifiers, microphone data or the user's media files.

Diagnostic logs remain on the device unless the user explicitly chooses to share an exported file through Android's share menu. The destination application selected by the user handles that file under its own privacy practices. Logs can be cleared from the app's Diagnostics screen and are removed when app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

Network access and service providers

The app connects to GuruBox and third-party audio hosting services to load and play content. Like ordinary internet services, those providers may receive technical request information such as an IP address, request time and device or browser information. Their handling of that information is governed by their own policies.

Permissions

The app uses internet access for streaming and Android's wake-lock and media-playback foreground-service capabilities so requested audio can continue playing. It does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS or call-log permissions.

External links

Links opened from GuruBox Radio use the device's browser or another external app. GuruBox Radio is not responsible for the content or privacy practices of external services.

Children

GuruBox Radio does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not provide chat, user-generated content, advertising or purchases.

Security and retention

No account database is maintained by the app. Local preferences and diagnostics remain on the device until cleared by the user, removed with app storage, or deleted when the app is uninstalled.

Changes

This policy may be updated when app features, service providers or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the latest revision.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the contact information published at gurubox.net.
